There are so many things you can do in Hershey, like going to Hershey Park, Chocolate World, the butterfly atrium at Hershey Gardens, and the Hershey Spa! Hershey Park has a unique event near Christmas time! They put up Christmas lights and give out hot chocolate, making it all the more festive. They offer special after-hours in the park, letting you stay and ride the rides and enjoy the lights. Next to Hershey Park is Chocolate World! There’s a small ride that tells you the process of making Hershey’s chocolate. You can make your own Hershey bar and add all your favorite toppings to make it delicious. There are so many sweets to choose from . There's also a trolley tour that you can go on that will tell you about the Hershey family. Interestingly, they had no children of their own but funded a boarding school for students in need. If you’re looking for a calm and relaxing day, I would recommend you go to the butterfly atrium, where you can check out some amazingly different types of butterflies, one might even land on you. You can also go on a walk in the gardens. Now, if you don’t feel like seeing insects and just want to relax, the spa is for you. It has beautiful decor and lots of space, and it's peaceful and relaxing. You can get a facial or a massage with cocoa-scented products. While you wait for your spa service, they have Hershey muffins and hot cocoa for you to enjoy. The staff is really nice and sweet.
